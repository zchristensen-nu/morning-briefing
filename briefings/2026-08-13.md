[Student loan delinquencies starting to stabilize, New York Fed says](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2026/08/11/student-loan-delinquencies-starting-stabilize-new-york-fed-says/)

[Departing UNCF President Reflects on How HBCUs Have Changed](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/institutions/minority-serving-institutions/2026/08/12/departing-uncf-president-reflects-how)

**Christian Science Monitor**

[Finish degrees for less: Schools overseas pitch US community college students](https://www.csmonitor.com/USA/Education/2026/0812/community-college-study-abroad)

Facing tuition hikes and credit-transfer hurdles at home, U.S. community college students are increasingly being recruited by overseas universities offering cheaper, faster paths to a bachelor's degree.

**Bloomberg**

[College Grads Struggle to Find Jobs. Non-Grads Are Giving Up](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2026-08-12/college-grads-struggle-to-find-jobs-non-grads-are-giving-up) (Op-ed)

A Bloomberg Opinion column argues that while college graduates are struggling in a weak job market, workers without degrees are faring even worse — eroding one of the strongest arguments for a four-year degree.
