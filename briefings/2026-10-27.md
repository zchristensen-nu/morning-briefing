**Higher Ed News**

**The Washington Post**
[A judge barred West Point from reviewing faculty speech. The academy kept doing it.](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/10/26/west-point-still-reviews-faculty-speaking-requests-despite-judges-order/)
West Point continues to require top officials to sign off on professors' public-speaking requests despite a federal judge's September ruling that the policy is unconstitutional, according to documents the Post obtained.

**Newsweek**
[Microsoft's 60,000-Worker Buyout Adds to H-1B Visa Uncertainty](https://www.newsweek.com/microsoft-layoffs-buyout-h1b-visa-green-card-jobs-12621897)

**The New York Times**
[France Makes Concessions to Students, but Not Enough to Halt Protests](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/26/world/europe/france-student-protests.html)

**Forbes**
[After $30 Million Gift, DePaul University Renames Its Law School](https://www.forbes.com/sites/michaeltnietzel/2026/10/27/after-30-million-gift-depaul-university-renames-its-law-school/)
Alumni Robert and Joan Clifford's gift will rename the school the Robert A. Clifford College of Law and fund a trial advocacy center, scholarships and faculty fellowships.

**The New York Times**
[Ohio State Professor Who Tackled a Filmmaker on Campus Resigns](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/26/us/ohio-state-professor-tackled-filmmaker-resignation.html)

**Higher Ed Dive**
[Michigan public universities lost over 5,000 students in fall 2026](https://www.highereddive.com/news/michigan-public-universities-lost-over-5000-students-in-fall-2026/872805/)

**The Chronicle of Higher Education**
[Pomona College to Become 'Parent' of Struggling Claremont Graduate U.](https://www.chronicle.com/article/pomona-college-to-become-parent-of-struggling-claremont-graduate-u)

**The New York Times**
[U.C.L.A. Asks Judge to Dismiss Trump Lawsuit Over Antisemitism](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/27/us/ucla-trump-lawsuit-antisemitism-employees.html)

**Global Campus/Regional News**

**The Boston Globe** (Op-ed)
[Every small college needs a closure plan, just in case](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/26/opinion/college-closures-massachusetts-new-england/)
The piece argues Massachusetts should require every college with fewer than 4,500 students to file a confidential closure plan with the state, naming transfer destinations for each program.

**The Globe and Mail** (Op-ed)
[Pushed to pursue safe degrees, students are shunning the humanities. At what cost?](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/personal-finance/article-university-degrees-humanities-business-engineering-youth-employment/)

**Toronto Star**

**Tampa Bay Times**

**WUSF**
[UF adds early decision as admission option for fall 2027 applicants](https://www.wusf.org/education/2026-10-26/uf-adds-early-decision-as-admission-option-for-fall-2027-applicants)
