**Higher Ed News**

**Inside Higher Ed**

[DOJ Declares 3 Race-Based STEM Programs Unconstitutional](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/government/science-research-policy/2026/08/14/doj-declares-3-race-based-stem-programs)

The Justice Department's Office of Legal Counsel ruled that three long-running NSF programs supporting Hispanic-serving institutions and minority participation in STEM are unconstitutional and must be terminated, affecting about $104 million of NSF's STEM education budget.

**Bloomberg**

[Professor's Alleged Role in China Programs Leads to Ban From US Funding](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/chinese-talent-plans-brought-to-light-with-science-agency-probe-of-professor)

A former NSF program officer was barred from federal funding for six years after an inspector general found he oversaw research grants while failing to disclose his own participation in Chinese government talent programs, part of a wider federal push to sever US-China research ties.

**Washington Post**

[University of Michigan plans to mask grades for new students](https://www.washingtonpost.com/education/2026/08/11/university-michigan-plans-mask-grades-new-students/)

Michigan's College of Literature, Science, and the Arts will hide first-semester letter grades from transcripts for incoming freshmen starting fall 2027, a mental-health-driven pilot that has since drawn national debate and pushback.

**The New York Times**

[Is Activism Hurting Sociology? Sociologists Are Torn.](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/14/us/activist-sociology-independent.html)

A group of sociologists broke away from colleagues in protest over the discipline's handling of Israel-related activism, reigniting debate over whether the field has drifted too far toward advocacy.

**Inside Higher Ed**

[Troy Punishes Professor for Writing to Lawmakers on University Letterhead](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/08/14/troy-punishes-professor-using-university-letterhead)

Troy University disciplined a faculty member for writing to state lawmakers on university letterhead, the latest episode to draw academic-freedom scrutiny of administrative pressure on faculty speech at the Alabama institution.

**Inside Higher Ed**

[Stockton Pulls Genocide Reference After Donor Complains](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/faculty/academic-freedom/2026/08/14/stockton-pulls-genocide-reference-after-donor-complains)

Stockton University removed language describing the war in Gaza as a genocide from its Holocaust and genocide studies program website after a complaint from the university's largest donor.
