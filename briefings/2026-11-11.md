**Higher Ed News**

**The Guardian**

[AI has made it 'close to impossible' to assess university coursework, regulator says](https://www.theguardian.com/education/2026/nov/11/ai-close-to-impossible-assess-university-coursework-regulator)

England's Office for Students says universities should return to in-person exams and traditional presentations after lecturers said AI has made it nearly impossible to fairly grade coursework.

**Reuters**

IBM to end visa, green card sponsorship for most roles in its Consulting arm

IBM confirmed it will stop sponsoring H-1B visas and green cards for most of its roughly 150,000-person Consulting division, telling affected foreign workers to relocate abroad or leave by year's end — the latest major employer to retreat from visa sponsorship amid the Trump administration's crackdown.

**The New York Times**

[Richard Holmes, Who Integrated Mississippi State University, Has Died](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/10/obituaries/richard-holmes-mississippi-state-university-dead.html)

Holmes, who became the first Black student to enroll at Mississippi State in 1965, has died.

**Forbes**

Immigration Lawsuit Challenges DHS For Restricting Student Internships

A coalition of higher education groups, including the Association of American Universities, is suing DHS and ICE over new guidance narrowing which internships qualify for international students' Curricular Practical Training work authorization; a federal judge heard arguments on blocking the rule on Nov. 6.

**KQED**

[Judge Orders Release of UC Berkeley Student From ICE Custody After SFO Arrest](https://www.kqed.org/news/12108913/judge-orders-release-of-uc-berkeley-student-from-ice-custody-after-sfo-arrest)

A federal judge ordered ICE to immediately release Paula Valentina Albornoz Hernandez, a UC Berkeley business student detained at San Francisco International Airport, finding the agency denied her a constitutionally required bond hearing.

**Reuters**

Exclusive: PLA-linked Chinese university bought Nvidia H200 chips despite US blacklist, records show

A Reuters review of Chinese procurement records found Northwestern Polytechnical University, which has military ties and sits on the U.S. export blacklist, obtained restricted Nvidia AI chips through a domestic supplier and rented server access from other Chinese firms.

[Penn State wrestles with $124M budget hole](https://www.highereddive.com/news/penn-state-wrestles-with-124m-budget-hole/874301/)

Brown University posts $50.4M deficit as expenses outpace revenue

Brown's audited results for the fiscal year ending June 30 show a $50.4 million operating deficit, reversing a surplus the year before, as operating expenses rose 4.3% to $1.54 billion.
