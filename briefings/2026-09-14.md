**Higher Ed News**

**Inside Higher Ed**

[DOJ Sues All States With In-State Tuition Policies for Undocumented Students](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/government/courts/2026/09/14/doj-sues-all-states-state-tuition-noncitizens)

The Justice Department's lawsuit against Hawaii completes a months-long campaign that has now targeted every state offering in-state tuition to undocumented students, roughly 25 states plus Washington, D.C.

**Inside Higher Ed**

[5 Things to Know About New International Student Visa Limits](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/global/international-students-us/2026/09/14/key-questions-about-international-student-visa)

A Department of Homeland Security rule replacing indefinite "duration of status" with fixed visa terms takes effect September 15, and colleges are racing to brief international students and staff on what changes.

**The New York Times**

[How University Core Curriculums Are Getting Political](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/14/us/university-core-curriculum-texas-politics.html)

Political pressure and other factors are pushing colleges, especially in Texas, to swap out core requirements like sociology and climate change for civics and classics.

**Nature**

[NSF moves to overhaul funding approach in line with White House priorities](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02892-9)

The National Science Foundation is shifting to a "portfolio-based" grantmaking model built around four new White House-directed research initiatives, and staff worry the change will squeeze funding for core science.

**Bloomberg**

[China Strengthens Political Oversight of University Leaders](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-13/china-strengthens-political-oversight-of-university-leaders)

Beijing has revised leadership rules for universities and other public institutions to tighten Communist Party control and political vetting of appointees.

**Bloomberg**

[Stanford Proposes $100 Million Fund to Secure Expansion Deal](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-11/stanford-proposes-100-million-fund-to-secure-expansion-deal)

Stanford is offering Santa Clara County a $100 million community fund as part of a permit bid to add nearly 2 million square feet of academic space and thousands of student beds over the next two decades.

**The Wall Street Journal** (Op-ed)

[Higher Ed Tries to Dodge Donor Transparency](https://www.wsj.com/opinion/higher-ed-tries-to-dodge-donor-transparency-42bc825f?mod=rss_opinion)

The piece argues universities are resisting calls to disclose the names of foreign and domestic donors even though the public has a legitimate interest in knowing who funds them.

**The Washington Post** (Op-ed)

[College students, try studying something useless this fall](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2026/09/13/college-students-try-studying-something-useless-this-fall/)

With AI upending even "safe" majors like computer science, the columnist argues students should pick professors who teach them to think rather than chase a degree that promises guaranteed pay.

**The Guardian**

[Can you futureproof your career by choosing an AI-resistant degree?](https://www.theguardian.com/education/2026/sep/14/futureproof-your-career-by-choosing-an-ai-resistant-degree)

With AI-powered virtual employees looming, graduate-employment experts weigh in on which fields of study offer the best odds of staying human-staffed.

**Newsweek**

[Student Loan Forgiveness Would Get Easier, Quicker for Many Under New Bill](https://www.newsweek.com/student-loan-forgiveness-would-get-easier-quicker-for-many-under-new-bill-12433966)

A bipartisan House bill from Reps. Bill Foster and Brian Fitzpatrick would let payments under a broader range of repayment plans count toward Public Service Loan Forgiveness.

**The Boston Globe**

**The Boston Globe** (Op-ed)

[New England could become a college graveyard. Schools should join forces to avoid that fate.](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/09/13/opinion/new-england-colleges-new-consortium/)

**The Globe and Mail**

Canadian computer-science enrollment is diverging sharply by school as AI reshapes students' calculations about which majors still lead to jobs.

**The Globe and Mail**

Quebec's new secularism law bans prayer rooms and collective religious practice at public universities and transit agencies, drawing objections from religious and civil-liberties groups.

**WUSF**

**CBC**
